United Kingdom of Moopsioid

About Moops

Moops is a country that has its own currency, and military. This country is governed by a president and 4 other higher-ups: Vice President (Benjamin), Secretary (Jack), Treasurer (Andrew), and Secretary of Commerce(Junsuke).

Military

The leader or fleet admiral of the army is Hunter. The military is having a war against the Muzz clan (Turkish for banana.)

Treasury

The treasury hasn’t been able to give people pay-day via cash, because it is a long and grueling process neither the Treasurer or Vice President wants to do. The treasury is currently at 120k moops only because of the 2 mooper (20k) and the 10 mooper (100k.) There are now credit cards in the United Kingdom of Moopsioid which has been a great addition to Moops because of how easy it is to transfer currency - there are 4 different types of credit cards: Yellow, Red, Blue, and White. All of them have a different interest rate, and have a different way of earning them.
